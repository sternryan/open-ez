# Rotax 915 iS and 916 iS: figures used in the Mod-EZ powerplant record

Condensed 2026-10-18 from a public-web research run (four long notes, all with URLs) and re-checked by a different method
(plain HTTP fetch, text extraction, search for the value; see `docs/harness/hangar-sweep-spotcheck.md`). Only figures that
were re-checked are used in the record. Public sources only.

## Weight and what it includes

| Item | 915 iS | 916 iS | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maker product page, engine with gearbox, turbocharger, intercooler, exhaust | 82.2 kg (181.2 lb), gearbox i = 2,54 | 85.8 kg (189 lb), gearbox i = 2,54 | flyrotax product pages |
| EASA data sheet E.121 Issue 17, dry weight, "iSc2 A" | 84,6 kg (186.51 lb) | 85,8 kg (189.16 lb) | EASA download 7633, section 5 |
| Installation manual, base engine with gearbox | 74.23 kg | 75.44 kg (166.3 lb) | IM-915 i, IM-916 i |
| Add-ons the maker lists separately (frame, external alternator, fuel pumps) | 2.0 + 3.0 + 1.6 kg | same | product pages |

The EASA dry weight includes the gearbox, turbocharger, cooling baffle, wiring, sensors, ECU, fusebox, oil tank, intercooler
and exhaust, and excludes fuel pumps, radiator and oil cooler. The record's installed weight is that figure plus frame, external
alternator and fuel pumps (91.2 kg for the 915, 92.4 kg for the 916), the same basis as the 912 iS build-up. Coolant, radiator
and oil cooler are not in any of these figures and stay unknown. The 82.2 kg product-page line uses a smaller include list and
is not comparable to the data sheet.

## Fuel flow

The operators manuals print an engine deck (rpm, throttle, altitude, power in kW, fuel flow in **kg/h**) and no density or L/h.
No row is labelled 75, 65 or 55 percent power. Rows used (5500 rpm, sea level):

- 915 iS: 87,47 kW burns 26,53 kg/h (about 303 g/kWh); 100 percent throttle 99,17 kW burns 32,16 kg/h.
- 916 iS: 89,96 kW burns 22,48027 kg/h (250 g/kWh, printed in the deck).

The record scales one row to the 52.0 kW mission power at constant specific consumption and converts kg/h to gal/h at the
record's 5.87 lb/gal. That gives about 5.9 gal/h (915) and 4.9 gal/h (916), flagged derived. Specific consumption is likely
higher at low power, so both understate the fuel flow. Take-off and continuous ratings: 915 104 kW at 5800 rpm and 99 kW at
5500; 916 117 kW and 101 kW.

## TBO

915 iS and iSc: 1200 h or 15 years, whichever comes first (maintenance manual, 2021 edition; the product page also says
1,200 hrs). 916 iS and iSc: 2000 h or 15 years (maintenance manual, December 2025; product page 2,000 hrs). 912 iS: 2000 h.

## Not found

Price on any maker page; fuel flow in L/h for any deck row; coolant and radiator installed weight; a flown Long-EZ with a 915
or 916 (a 915 iS installation in a Long-EZ is documented as engine-mounted in a builder log, with no flight report found).
