This note was written from pages opened on 20 October 2026. It sits beside the 18 October 2026 laminating-resin note and does not re-argue what that note already settled: plans-era Safe-T-Poxy practice, the coupon method behind the 20 April 2018 *Kitplanes* comparison, and the process sheets already recorded there for Aeropoxy, West System, and Jeffco. It corrects that note where a 20 October page shows a wrong attribution. The airplane is an open-source Long-EZ derivative, foam-core and wet-layup glass. ([Laminating resin MGS L 285, Hexion, footer August 2005 / January 2006, p. 1.1-14](https://www.aircraftspruce.com/catalog/pdf/mgs285tech.pdf); [EPIKOTE Resin MGS LR285, Momentive file named 20140402, p. 5; the date is the file name, not a printed revision line](https://www.globalhobby.ru/manual/LR285.pdf))

## 2. Strongest published dossier for a tank interior

**E-Z 10A with E-Z 87B** has the strongest published dossier for an avgas or UL94 tank interior, and the dossier still contains no UL94 immersion. On the only head-to-head 100LL test, Stewart reports no visible brand difference among E-Z 10/87, MGS 285/287, and Jeffco 1307/3176, and he says the set has no statistical significance. E-Z is not ahead of MGS on a 100LL measurement. The extra E-Z pages are the Australia FAQ and the Schroeder sidebar: qualitative studies of 83B, 87B, and 92B in gasoline with 10 percent ethanol, diesel with 5 percent biodiesel, and isopropyl/xylene. Those pages do not name avgas or UL94, and they print no uptake percent. The only opened numeric gasoline result, +0.379 percent in 28 days, is CoPoxy 100A with E-Z 92B in fuel labeled Gasoline. The August 2007 E-Z 10A bulletin prints handling data and no fuel row. PAFI-MTP-002 names EZ10A with EZ87B for a future 100LL screen and prints no result. ([Eric Stewart, *Kitplanes*, 20 April 2018, “Results”](https://www.kitplanes.com/rapid-prototyping-and-experimental-design-17/); [Aircraft Spruce Australia E-Z Poxy FAQ, viewed 20 October 2026](https://www.aircraftspruce.com.au/catalog/cmpages/ezpoxy.php); [100A Resin / E-Z 92B, Endurance Technologies, revised May 2012](https://polytek.com/content/pdf/cpd/TDB/100a_e-z_92b.pdf); [E-Z 10A technical data bulletin, revised August 2007](https://www.aircraftspruce.com/catalog/pdf/EZpoxytechbulletin.pdf); [PAFI-MTP-002, 15 May 2024, Appendix I](https://flyeagle.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/PAFI-MTP-002.pdf))

## Recommendation

**Unknown.** There is no UL94 immersion that clears MGS L285/H287, and there is no UL94 immersion that shows a replacement is required.
