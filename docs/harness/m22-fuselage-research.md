## Conflicts

- **Stations:** no contradiction with the model. Side length 103 from F22 lands on `fs_firewall`
  125, and the panel is 39.75 against the manual's 40. The main gear at 110.5 sits aft of the rear
  bulkhead's bottom, and the strake LE at 50 falls inside the side span. Length 201.4 and `fs_tail`
  stay unresolved; chapters 4–6 end at FS 125.
- **Occupant arms are not bulkheads.** `fs_pilot_seat` 59 and `fs_rear_seat` 103 are CG arms. The
  seat bulkheads run FS 63.6–81.75 and 107–118.5. The "59.75" and "103" on p36 are side-foam
  coordinates, not these arms.
- **Internal to the plans:**
  - The 8 in dish is "0.5 in deep" in the text but 0.3 in the section drawing.
  - p38 prints MS21042-4, which CP29 LPC 67 changes.
  - The plans text says no slurry, which the Canard Pusher overrides.

## Judgement

**Book-true from pages the owner holds:**
- the side panels (outline, top at W.L. 23, bulkhead and cutout positions);
- the front and rear seat bulkheads.

**Representational, because they exist only on full-size A-sheets:**
- longeron placement;
- the instrument panel, F22, F28 and firewall outlines;
- the gear-extrusion positions.

Also representational: the bottom-block contour (partial) and the rear bulkhead's top width
(unprinted). F22 at FS 22 is inferred from printed coordinates, so it is flagged `derived`.
