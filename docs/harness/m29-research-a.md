# Covers, fairings, consoles (chapter 24, p159 to p161): source research (2026-10-04)

Read-only research pass for milestone 2.9, crew A. Every number below was read on the page image (hand digits cropped
and enlarged 2.5x to 5x), not the OCR. 1980 plans cited as `plans-1980:pNNN` (PDF page). Cobelu chapter 24 text (`cobelu`)
was diffed against the scan; scan wins. CP text cited as `cp-text:CPnn p<page>` (+ LPC number). Owner's Manual cited as
`om-1980:p<printed>`. Paraphrases are ten words or fewer. Nothing in the repo was edited. The owner holds NO A-sheets:
the thigh-support rib curve ("see A8"), the instrument panel drawing (A1), and the brake/console location drawings are
not available, so every shape that lives only there is marked "repr".

## 0. Answers first (read this)

1. Chapter 24 is plans-1980:p159 to p161 (printed 24-1 to 24-3; p161 footer says last page of chapter 24; the contents
   page plans-1980:p2 also lists 3 pages). Chapter 25 starts p162. Five steps: lower aft cover, left consoles, front
   thigh support (floor plus 2 ribs plus fuel-valve cover), canard cover, wing/centre-section gap seal; plus a radio
   support note on p161.
2. The wing/spar gap seal that chapter 19 defers is step 5 (plans-1980:p161). Chapter 19 points to it twice: a
   handwritten note on p134 and "see chap 24" on the section C-C drawing on p129. Chapter 23 also defers cowl-cutout
   leakage sealing to it (cobelu ch23). The gap is about 1/2 in (glyph read, medium); wedge foam, micro, 1 ply BID,
   1/16 in gap left at the front, silicone after paint.
3. Chapter 24 prints NO weights and NO new fuselage stations beyond LC1 at FS 52 to FS 60. Every chapter-24 mass in the
   model must be derived (section 2f) or taken from CP26 p3 (the 183 lb fuselage "includes side consoles").
4. Ledger traps: the 183 lb fuselage row already contains the consoles; the lower aft cover, thigh support, canard
   cover, seal foam, silicone and paint are in none of the CP26 p3 rows (section 7). No arm is printed for any of
   them.
5. One real conflict: how many BID plies the lower aft cover takes (scan hand-edit versus cobelu, section 5, C1).

## 5. Conflicts

C1. Lower aft cover ply count. plans-1980:p159 hand-edits the outside paragraph from two plies to one and blanks the
inside paragraph's ply word; cp-text:CP28 p8 LPC #54 says "3rd paragraph, one ply" (which is the inside paragraph by
my count). cobelu keeps inside 1 ply and outside 2 plies (total 3). The scan gives inside unreadable, outside 1.
Resolution needs a page: the owner's edit may be later than LPC #54. Model carries 2 or 3 plies as an open flag; mass
effect about 0.23 lb on an 18 x 20 in plan area.

C2. LC2 length 30.6 versus an early read of 30.8. Final 4x read favours 30.6 (open-topped last digit); both are within
0.2 in; no second source.

C3. cobelu adds an LB13 slot (0.25 in wide, FS 48.9 to FS 56.5, 1.25 in from wall) that is not on the scan pages or in
the CP text I searched. A later edition or a plans revision; scan cannot confirm. Do not model it as book.

C4. Wing seal gap size: both plans-1980:p161 and cobelu read 1/2 in; the printed glyph is a stacked fraction I cannot
distinguish from 3/4 at this scan resolution. Medium confidence.
