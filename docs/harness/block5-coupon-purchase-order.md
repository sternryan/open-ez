**Resin.**

- About 6.8 kg of cloth is wetted. At roughly 1:1 resin to cloth by weight, plus 25% mixing loss,
  that comes to about 8.5 kg of mixed system: 6.1 kg resin and 2.4 kg hardener at 100:40.
- One gallon of LR 285 is about 4.5 kg. That figure is not read from the TDS.
- So 2 gallons of resin and 3 quarts of LH 287 are needed. The vendor states about 2.5 lb per quart,
  so 3 quarts is about 3.4 kg.
