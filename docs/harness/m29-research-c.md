# Upholstery (chapter 26), back cover, and the Owner's Manual weight and balance: source research (2026-10-04)

Read-only research pass for milestone 2.9, crew C. Chapter 26 and the back cover were read on the page images (hand digits
cropped and enlarged 2x to 5x). The Owner's Manual (OM) was read from the PDF text layer and the tables and charts were checked
on rendered page images (OM PDF page = printed page). 1980 plans cited as `plans-1980:pNNN` (PDF page). CP text cited as
`cp-text:CPnn p<page>` (+ LPC number). Paraphrases are ten words or fewer. Nothing in the repo was edited. The owner holds NO
A-sheets; chapter 26 has none, so every cushion, headrest and suitcase shape beyond the printed outline is "repr".

## 0. Answers first (read this)

1. Chapter 26 is ONE page: plans-1980:p170, printed "26-1", marked last page of plans. p171 is the back-cover 3-view (no printed
   page number). Chapter 26 prints NO weights, NO fuselage stations and NO materials list. Only cushion, headrest and suitcase
   outline sizes. No upholstery mass or arm exists in any held source.
2. The OM sample empty airplane (730 lb, FS 111.7, moment 81541; om-1980:p25, p35) does not add up on its own table: the sample
   weighing rows net to 727.9 lb, not 730 (section 5, C1). The moments do close to 81541 once the printed left-main moment typo
   (4110) is read as 41106. Ledger closure should say which of 727.9 or 730 it targets (the CG 111.7 comes from 730).
3. The OM itself places the light-pilot sample OUTSIDE the envelope (103.96 against aft limit 103, om-1980:p25), though the chart
   plots its dot near FS 102.8 (C3). Only the heavy-pilot sample (1323 lb, FS 101.06) is inside 97 to 103. An exit test that
   demands both samples inside the envelope cannot pass on book numbers. Suggested wording: heavy inside, light reproduces its
   printed 103.96 (aft of limit).
4. Envelope (om-1980:p28): normal region FS 97.0 to 103.0 up to 1325 lb; takeoff-only hatch above 1325 up to 1425 lb, its right
   edge at FS 104; aft-of-limit hatch FS 104 to 105 for 1325 down to 900. Chart floor 900 lb, top 1450 lb.
5. Missing or unsourced masses and arms are listed per ledger row in section 7. Biggest gaps: engine (unsourced, arm wrong),
   electrical split (battery nose, starter/alternator FS 150+), finish/filler/paint (no row at all; CP gives 12 to 85 lb range),
   upholstery and seat belts (no weight in any held source), instruments, cowl (CP27 weights, no arm), wing and canard
   (arms unsourced, weights conflict).

## 8. Conflicts

C1. OM sample weighing arithmetic: the four weight rows net to 727.9, the printed empty weight is 730 (om-1980:p35, p36). The
    moments reproduce 81541 (with 41106 for the left main, printed 4110). The empty CG 111.7 is 81541/730. Either use 730 as the
    book target (matches the sample loading rows, which all use 730) and flag the 2.1 lb gap as a book arithmetic slip, or build
    from 727.9 and get CG 112.0. Needs the captain's ruling; no page settles it.
C2. Empty CG 111.7 is aft of the loaded aft limit 103 (om-1980:p25, p28). Not a conflict, but an easy misread (also in m28).
C3. Light-pilot sample: printed 103.96 at 1113 lb (om-1980:p25, p26) versus the chart dot near FS 102.8 at 1100 lb (om-1980:p28).
    The p25 prose also says the sample is outside the 103 aft limit while the chart dot sits on the line.
C4. Back-cover nose wheel WL: printed "-25" struck out and "-22" in ink (plans-1980:p171) versus CP25 LPC #24 saying the printed
    value was -23 (cp-text:CP25 p6). Either the CP misquoted the printed value or the scan shows a different edition. Final -22
    agrees in both.
C5. Nose wheel station: FS 17 (plans-1980:p171) versus "about 20" with 19.6 in the sample (om-1980:p35). Already in the ledger as a
    conflict pair.
C6. Takeoff-only gross: 1425 lb (om-1980:p4, p5, p28) versus 1420 lb (om-1980:p36). The chart note on p28 says "see page 36" while
    the chart title cites "see page 39". The OM's own conditions on p36 to p37 are the only rule text.
C7. LPC #39 names "Long-EZ canopy (Chapter 10)" (cp-text:CP26 p6) but this plans edition numbers the canopy chapter 18 (cobelu
    chapter list) and the p170 text still reads "Chap 22". Canopy chapter-number references are unreliable across editions.
C8. Weight sources for the canopy and aileron: CP26 p3 (16 lb, 5.125 lb, uncured) versus CP27 p1 (17 lb, 5.4 lb, filled and
    painted). Not a conflict (different states) but both should be labelled by finish state.
C9. Wing: model 85 lb for both wings against 120 to 128 lb from CP27 p1 and CP26 p3 (already flagged in config).
C10. Engine: 243 lb vs 249.1 lb (113 kg) vs book limit 246 lb; all three disagree (already flagged).
C11. p28 chart caption: the OM front matter says the manual was updated through CP82 (om-1980:p2) while the sample weights come
    from a much earlier edition; sample numbers cannot be assumed to reflect any particular airplane.
