## Review Focus (each pinned by a test)

1. **A representational part shown as book.** The strut, extrusions, canard cutout, roll-over tilt and
   gear track are fitted. Expectation: every new solid carries a fidelity; a test fails on a missing
   tag or a book/derived tag without a valid citation; the lab stripes and labels them (e2e).
2. **The axle drifting off its printed station, or the track measured off an image.** Expectation: a
   test pins the axle point at `fs_spar_aft_face − 15 = 110.5` and W.L. −22 with book provenance; the
   track is a representational constant with no `book`/`derived` provenance anywhere, and the lateral
   tip-over check returns "not yet computed".
3. **The gear lump surviving.** Expectation: `landing_gear_arm_in` 84.5 and `landing_gear_weight_lb` 45
   are gone as free constants; the physics weight items are the decomposed rows; a test asserts the
   main strut row is 22 lb at 110.5 citing p50/p8, and that every unsourced row is flagged.
4. **Ground handling invented.** Expectation: the readout note states F.S. 110.5 and the 12° tip-back
   line, and the tip-back grade is "not yet computed"; a test fails if a numeric tip-back or tip-over
   verdict appears.
5. **The bank rolls wrong-way or out of order.** Expectation: `f07.skin-right` precedes `f07.skin-left`
   in the graph (test walks it); the lab's roll at skin-right puts the right side up (a sign test on
   the pure pose function), and the inversion happens at `f09.position-gear`.
6. **The skin schedule drifting.** Expectation: a test asserts the third ply ends at the front seat
   bulkhead and the 3-ply strip spans F.S. 60–110.
7. **Canard and ch4–6 regressing.** Expectation: their lab e2e assertions are unchanged (diff of the
   assertion lines against ec7ca75 at each lab commit).
8. **The nose-wheel conflict lost.** Expectation: `fs_nose_wheel` carries `conflict` status with the
   printed 17 and the manual's 20; a test asserts it.
