# Canopy (chapter 18): source research (2026-10-03)

Read-only research pass for milestone 2.6. Every cited value was read on the page image (crops enlarged
2x for hand digits), not the OCR. 1980 plans cited as `plans-1980:pNNN` (PDF page). Cobelu text
(`cobelu`, ch18) was diffed against the scan; scan wins. CP text cited as `cp-text:CPnn p<page>` (issue
page from the sectioned text footers). Owner's Manual as `om-1980:p<printed>`. Paraphrases are ten
words or fewer. The captain re-verifies any value that goes into the model. Nothing in the repo was
edited.

### 2.6 Inside foam carving, pad layout (step 7, p112) (all stations measured from FS 117 forward)

The plan view from below prints a reference "FS 117" at the aft end (the last digit is a faint "7"
that looks like a "2"; CP27 LPC 43 says "FS 117 ... see page 18-5", which resolves it. Also see the
arithmetic check below). All eight reinforcement pads are 2.5 in long (printed). Dimensions run to the
**aft edge** of each pad (checked: each arrow tip lands on the aft edge of its pad).

| Pad | Printed distance from FS 117 to pad aft edge | Pad span in FS (derived) | Conf |

| Left middle latch pad | 41.0 | FS 73.5 to 76.0 | high |
| Left safety-catch pad | 59.0 | FS 55.5 to 58.0 (centre 56.75) | high |
| Left front latch pad | 71.0 | FS 43.5 to 46.0 | high |
| Right aft hinge, aft pad | 20.0 | FS 94.5 to 97.0 | high |
| Right aft hinge, fwd pad | 25.5 | FS 89.0 to 91.5 | high |
| Right fwd hinge, aft pad | 48.0 | FS 66.5 to 69.0 | high |
| Right fwd hinge, fwd pad | 53.5 | FS 61.0 to 63.5 | high |

Right forward hinge arithmetic: pad aft edge FS
117 - 48.0 = 69.0, pad spans FS 66.5 to 69.0; second pad aft edge FS 117 - 53.5 = 63.5, spans FS 61.0 to
63.5. So hinge B covers FS 61.0 to 69.0 and hinge A covers FS 89.0 to 97.0 (each 8.0 long, which is
exactly the "8" dimension printed on the pad sketch on p113: 28.0 - 20.0 = 8.0 and 56.0 - 48.0 = 8.0).
Hinge A to firewall "20 in" and hinge B "48 in" on p114 are the same two numbers (the pad aft edges).
CP27 LPC 43 corrects "to firewall bulkhead" to "to aft end of canopy frame FS 117" (two places).

Cross-checks supporting FS 117 as the datum:
- Safety-catch pad centre: 117 - 59 - 1.25 = 56.75, and p116 prints the SC-1 screw "at F.S. 56.75".
  CP25 p3 says FS 57 (new construction). Exact agreement. **High**.
- Latch pad centres (derived): 104.75, 74.75, 44.75 against the p117 labels FS 104, FS 74, FS 44 (0.75
  off; the labels are rounded, or refer to a forward pad edge: forward edges are 103.5, 73.5, 43.5). The
  labels are within 0.75 in of the pad, medium.
- Frame length: aft end FS 117; most forward pad edge FS 43.5; front cut about FS 41.65 (derived from
  p111). Foam frame about 75 in from front cut to rear cut (derived, medium).

## 5. Conflicts and unsourced items

- **Canopy outline and bubble contour (unsourced).** The plans print only the trim view with 68 in
  length, 0.5 and 0.75 offsets. The bubble itself is a vendor part with no printed section.
  The captain must use a **representational** canopy (loft from the printed frame constraints: top
  WL at least 36.5 at 6 in forward of the headrest, WL 35.3 at FS 110, nose tip about FS 44.75,
  rear cut at FS 117, hinge side right).
- **FS 117 reading on p112.** The plan view label looks like "FS 112" at first sight; the last glyph
  is a faint "7". Resolved by: p111 prints FS 117 and 8 to the firewall; CP27 LPC 43 says FS 117; the
  safety-catch centre arithmetic 117 - 59 - 1.25 = 56.75 equals the printed F.S. 56.75. **Use 117.**
- **Front cut reference (8.8 and 14.0).** Datum not named. Read as F28 (FS 27.65); gives cut FS 36.45 at
  the top and 41.65 at the longeron, but only medium confidence. Model as representational.
- **Latch pad centres vs p117 labels.** Derived 104.75, 74.75, 44.75 versus printed 104, 74, 44.
  Unresolved by 0.75 in; the latch labels may be to pad forward edges (103.5, 73.5, 43.5).
- **Hinge pad dimensions.** Interpretation "arrow tip on aft edge of pad" is mine, validated by
  the "8" overall pad dimension on p113 and by the 56.75 check on the left side. Right-side hinge pad
  tips: the 25.5 arrow lands on the aft edge of the forward pad of hinge A.
- **Hinge side.** "4 right pads only" (p113) and "top view of right longeron" (p114): hinges are on
  the right; latches on the left (door on the left; C1-L handle on the left). The Owner's Manual does not
  say which side. Medium-high.
- **Hinge number P6 vs the OM.** OM says "check hinges" only. No weight or length for MS20001-P6 is
  printed. The hinge length is 8.0 in per pair of pads (derived from pad stations); hinge length itself
  unsourced.
- **Safety catch FS.** CP25 says 57; scan says 56.75. Compatible; keep 56.75.
- **Layup, "last 3 plies".** p110 text says the first ply goes into the groove and the last 3 over the
  top. Total side plies are 4 (2 BID + 2 UND); front and rear 3. Consistent. Not a conflict.
- **Pad reinforcement "15 plies".** The p113 sketch shows about 7 line pairs; text says 15 plies of BID.
  Use 15 (text). Medium on whether it means 15 plies per pad.
- **Rear foam width ("Approx 10 in") and 6 in rear cut at the top.** Medium.
- **Wood inserts under pads.** Appears only in the Solitaire SPC 20. Long-EZ p113 sketches show a
  "joggle" and flox pads, no plywood. Do not model.
- **Plexiglass thickness, weight and tint.** Not in the plans. The OM is silent. Unsourced.
- **Hinge and latch pad station numbers vs "A 20 in, B 48 in to firewall bulkhead".** The printed text
  would put hinge A at FS 105 if read from the firewall. CP27 LPC 43 removes this. Use FS 117.
- **Door position.** 3 in aft of the instrument panel and 3 in below the longeron top are printed, but
  the panel station is itself a conflict in the model (39.75 against the manual's 40). Door sits at about
  FS 43 to 47 (derived, low).
- **Canopy geometry cross-check from the model.** Roll-over peak WL 35.6 at FS 79.05 (config) against the
  canopy requirement: top at least WL 36.5 at 6 in forward of the headrest. The roll-over canopy insert
  (1.5 in below the peak, ch8) is where the canopy frame bears on the roll-over structure.

## Judgement

**Book (high):** 68 in plexiglass length, 0.5 and 0.75 trim offsets, A 13.5 and B 12.3 checks, five-ply
schedule (sides 2 BID + 2 UND, ends 3 BID), 0.06 in foam relief, rear cut FS 117 (8 in to firewall), pad
stations (FS 117 less 11, 41, 59, 71 and 20, 25.5, 48, 53.5; 2.5 long), safety catch at FS 56.75, door
4.3 x 3.7, hinge count and hardware, vent and brace tube parts, 15 BID plies per pad, 48 h cure.

**Derived (medium):** hinge spans FS 89 to 97 and 61 to 69; latch pad centres; front cut about FS 41.65;
brace tube forward FS 82; frame length.

**Representational:** the bubble contour and frame outer surface, the four temporary blocks (full-size
outline only), the vent teardrop, door position, the front and rear covers' profile (carved to the
fuselage), linkage geometry on the full-size latch drawing, canopy opening arc (15 degrees past
vertical is the only dimension).

**Top model-bound values:** rear cut FS 117 (high, three-way cross-check), hinge pad stations
(high on the printed numbers, medium on the aft-edge reading), SC-1 FS 56.75 (high), A 13.5 and B 12.3
(high), layup 2 BID + 2 UND sides and 3 BID ends (high), canopy mass 16 lb (sourced, N26MS).
